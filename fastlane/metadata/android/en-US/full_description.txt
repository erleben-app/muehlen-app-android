Mühlen.app brings historic mills to your phone, with their history, audio guides and 360° tours. The first region is the Minden-Lübbecke district in Germany, with 43 mills: windmills and watermills, post mills, horse mills, the ship mill in Minden and the Wehe mill site with its bakehouse, crafts museum and miller's house.

What you will find in the app:

• the history of every mill
• 42 audio guides, to listen to on site or at home
• 30 tours in 360°
• 42 quizzes
• 13 then-and-now photo comparisons
• photo galleries
• all content stored on the device, downloaded automatically on Wi-Fi, so the app works without mobile reception
• a map of all mills with directions to each one
• the Destel gallery windmill as a 3D model you can turn around

Texts, pictures and audio guides come from the Mühlenverein im Kreis Minden-Lübbecke e.V. (mill association), those for the Wehe mill site from the Heimatverein Wehe e.V. The app is published by erleben.app.

The app is free, shows no ads and needs no account. The interface is available in German and English; texts and audio guides are mostly in German.

More districts and groups of mills will follow.
